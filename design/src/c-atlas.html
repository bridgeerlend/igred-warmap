About this mockup. The incidents are real — one live day of the GDELT feed, clustered by this project's own pipeline. The landmass is Natural Earth 1:110m (public domain), drawn as a stipple in the Natural Earth projection; no third-party tiles are used anywhere. Two things stand in for parts not yet built: the set of countries counted as actively at war is a placeholder until the UCDP register is connected, and the magenta points are illustrative — nothing here has yet been verified by hand.
